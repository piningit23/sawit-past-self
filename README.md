Two Lines to Your Past Self — a program that writes exactly two lines to `/output/entry.txt`: a warning to its past self about one specific moment (a 2 a.m. text, 3 March 2021), and one sentence on why that moment mattered and what changed because of it.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
